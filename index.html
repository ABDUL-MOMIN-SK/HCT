Regulations

Tournament Rules

01

Match Format

Every match is 15 overs per side, played with a red ball. The tournament follows a knockout format.

02

Team Size

Each team plays 11 players on the field.

03

Squad List & Replacements

  • Submit a squad list of 15 players before your first match.
  • After the first match, each player replaced with a new player carries a fine of ₹999, payable to the organiser.
  • A substitute fielder allowed by the umpire does not count as a replacement.
04

GP-Level Teams

All players of a team must belong to the same Gram Panchayat (GP).

05

Meghalaya Teams

Meghalaya teams are formed by MLA constituency. All players must belong to the same constituency.

06

Organisation Teams

Any club or organisation may form a team and take part.

07

Residency Rule

A player who has lived in a GP for more than 15 years is treated as a player of that GP. The same applies to an MLA constituency for Meghalaya teams.

08

Overseas Players

  • Each team may include up to 3 overseas players.
  • Assam teams: overseas players from Assam only.
  • Meghalaya teams: overseas players from Assam and the selected region of Meghalaya only.
09

Registration

The registration fee is ₹5,555 per team. Entry is confirmed once the fee is paid.

10

Ball Charge

Each team pays a ball charge of ₹200 to the organiser before the start of every match.

11

Late Arrival

  • Report at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start. The toss is held 15 minutes before the start.
  • Up to 10 minutes late: one over is reduced per side.
  • More than 20 minutes late: the match is awarded to the opposing team.
  • Requests to change match timings will not be accepted.
12

Minimum Players

  • A team needs at least 7 players present to start the match.
  • If a team cannot field 7 players, the match is awarded to the opposing team.
  • The remaining players must arrive within 5 overs. If they do not, only the 7 who took the field may bat.
13

Toss & Tie-break

  • The toss is held by the umpires with both captains present. The winner chooses to bat or bowl first.
  • A tied match is decided by a Super Over.
  • In the Super Over, the team that batted second bats first. Each team picks 3 batters and 1 bowler.
  • If the Super Over is also tied, the winner is the team with the most sixes in the main match, then the most fours, then the fewest wickets lost.
14

Bowler’s Over Limit

  • A bowler may bowl a maximum of 3 overs in a match, and not two overs in a row.
  • If a bowler is injured and the umpire finds him unfit to continue, any player who has not bowled the maximum overs may complete the over.
15

Playing Conditions

  • Each team must field a wicketkeeper.
  • Byes, leg byes, overthrows and LBW apply.
  • Neutral umpires officiate all matches. The umpire’s decision is final.
16

Dress Code

  • Wear a matching team uniform with a jersey number. A player without the team jersey will not be allowed to play.
  • Wear sports shoes. Barefoot, slippers and spike shoes are not allowed.
17

Conduct & Discipline

  • Respect the umpires, opponents and spectators. The captain is responsible for the spirit of the game.
  • A team that argues at length or refuses to continue may forfeit the match, on the decision of the umpires and organisers.
  • A team that walks off in protest, or abuses the umpires on or off the field, will be disqualified.
  • Play Fair, Respect All, Enjoy the Game.
18

Player Eligibility

  • A valid Voter ID is mandatory for verification.
  • A player without a Voter ID must show a residence certificate issued by the concerned authority.
  • A player may play for only one team in the tournament.
  • A team that fields an ineligible player may be disqualified by the organising committee.
19

General Provisions

  • First aid will be available on the ground. The organisers are not liable for any injury.
  • In matters not covered by these rules, the tournament committee’s decision is final.
  • The committee may amend any rule at any stage of the tournament.
Teams may be asked to show ID or residence proof of players to verify GP, constituency and overseas status.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who counts as an overseas player?
  • A player who does not belong to the team’s own GP (or MLA constituency for Meghalaya teams). Anyone who has lived or stayed in a GP or constituency for more than 15 years counts as belonging to it. A team may have up to three overseas players. Assam teams may take them from Assam only; Meghalaya teams may take them from Assam and the selected region of Meghalaya.
Can a club or organisation take part?
  • Yes. Any organisation can form a team and participate.
How many players can a team register?
  • 11 players play on the ground. Each team submits a squad list of 15 players before its first match. Each player replaced after the first match carries a fine of ₹999 payable to the organiser.
How do I confirm my registration?
  • Submit the form, pay the ₹5,555 fee and send the details and payment screenshot on WhatsApp.
How can I follow the matches live?
  • Download the Android app from the Play Store or scan the QR code at the bottom of this page.
